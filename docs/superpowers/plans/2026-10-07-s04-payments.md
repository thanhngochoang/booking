**Goal:** A customer reviews a booking (S04.03), sees "Thanh toán 100%", picks Chuyển khoản QR (default), MoMo or ZaloPay and pays the full price. For QR they land on S04.04: a VietQR with the amount and the content "PB XXXXXX", the collection account (masked), a 15-minute countdown, "Tạo QR mới" and "Mở app ngân hàng". The server learns about money only from a signed webhook (or the reconciliation job that asks the gateway), matches the transfer by its content, and applies the mis-transfer table: exact → paid; over → paid and the excess refunded; under → kept open until expiry, then everything refunded; after expiry → refunded; no match → `needs_review`. Everything runs against the fake gateway; no word "cọc" is left in the app.

**Architecture:** Pure rules go into `packages/domain`: the 100% policy, the transfer code (`PB` + 6 characters), the VietQR EMVCo payload, the transfer decision table, and the use cases `createPayment`, `handleTransferNotification`, `checkPayment` and `runPaymentSweeps`, all against an extended `PaymentGateway` port plus `BookingStore` queries. Signature checking needs `node:crypto`, which the domain purity test forbids, so it lives in Functions. The Functions side has an `onRequest` webhook (`paymentWebhook`, HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body for the fake provider) that verifies, stores the raw body and hands a `TransferNotification` to the domain. `confirmFakePayment` becomes a dev tool that signs a simulated transfer (exact, under, over or wrong content) and runs it through the same webhook pipeline in-process. A `paymentClock` schedule runs every 5 minutes. The app never reads `payments/*`: `createPayment` mirrors what S04.04 needs into `bookings/{id}.checkout`. S04.04 renders that mirror and waits on `bookings/{id}.status` as today.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (`packages/domain`, Functions v2 `onCall`/`onRequest`/`onSchedule`, `node --test` via tsx), Firestore rules/indexes. Flutter, Riverpod 3, go_router, `qr_flutter` ^4.1.0 (already in `pubspec.yaml`), `flutter_test` with the fakes in `test/support/`.

## Decisions (where the spec is silent or the code disagrees)

1. **Keep the stored field names `deposit`/`remaining` for this unit.** `deposit` now holds the full price and `remaining` is always 0. The policy constant becomes `UPFRONT_PERCENT = 100` (domain) and `BookingRules.upfrontPercent = 100` (Dart). Renaming the fields to `terms.price` and milestones (spec 10-05 §4.2) touches S05/S06/earnings and belongs with the milestone work. Every user-facing string drops "cọc" now. Arb keys keep their old names; only the values change. *If wrong:* one mechanical rename later.
2. **Milestones are not built here.** Escrow release stays as it is (whole payment after completion + 24 h). The two-milestone release needs the `shot`/`approved` statuses from the delivery spec (#130, #134). The copy already promises "2 mốc"; the ledger and the PR say so.
3. **Webhook signature (fake provider):** header `x-pb-signature: sha256=<hex>`, HMAC-SHA256 of the raw request body, compared with `timingSafeEqual`. Secret env: `FAKE_PAYMENT_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, a self-made test value in `.env.booking-c1922` and in a new emulator-only `.env.local`.
   - It is not a `defineSecret`, because a new secret would break the CI deploy until someone creates it.
   - The fake provider is refused (404) wherever `fakePaymentsAllowed()` is false. The real intermediary's scheme and secret come with its own unit.
   - The spec puts `verifyWebhook` on the port; verification lives in Functions because of the domain purity rule, and the domain receives an already verified `TransferNotification`.
4. **Webhook body (fake provider):** JSON `{ "txnId": string, "amount": int VND, "content": string, "receivedAt": ISO-8601 UTC, "payer"?: { "bankCode"?: string, "accountMasked"?: string, "name"?: string } }`. Idempotency key: `${provider}_${txnId}` = the id of `payment_notifications/{id}`, which stores `{provider, txnId, raw, receivedAt, outcome, paymentId?}`. Responses:
   - 200 `{ok:true,outcome}` when processed or a duplicate;
   - 401 for a bad or missing signature;
   - 400 for a bad body;
   - 404 for an unknown or disabled provider;
   - 500 for a transient failure (so the provider retries).
5. **Transfer code:** `"PB " + 6 chars` from the alphabet `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ` (no 0/O/1/I/L/U).
   - It is derived from the payment id by a pure 32-bit FNV-1a hash, so it works with any id format.
   - `createPayment` regenerates the id when an open (`created`) payment already uses the code, up to 3 tries.
   - **Matching:** uppercase the content, drop every non-`[A-Z0-9]`, and take the first `PB` + 6 alphabet chars that names an existing payment. This survives bank apps that strip spaces or add text.
6. **Underpaid then topped up:** transfers to one payment add up (`receivedAmount`). The payment becomes paid when the sum reaches the amount, and anything above it is refunded. Time decides: a transfer with `receivedAt >= expiresAt` is late even if the sweep has not marked the payment `expired` yet.
7. **A transfer to an already paid or refunded payment** (paid twice, or the old QR paid after "Tạo QR mới") is refunded in full with reason `extra_transfer`. "Tạo QR mới" marks the previous open payment `expired`; if that payment had received something, it is refunded with `underpaid_expired`.
8. **Refunds** go through the existing `Refund` + `gateway.issueRefund` with `refundId` as the idempotency key, against the provider transaction (the intermediary refunds to the sending account). New reasons: `overpaid_excess`, `underpaid_expired`, `late_transfer`, `extra_transfer`. Storing the sender's full account and the S22.04 confirmation sheet are deferred to #141 (S22). `payer` from the webhook is kept only inside the raw body.
9. **`needs_review`** is the outcome of an unmatched notification (stored on `payment_notifications`); nothing else happens until S23.02 (staff). `PaymentStatus.needs_review` is added to the enum for the spec but no code path writes it on a `Payment` in this unit. `failed` is dropped from `PAYMENT_STATUSES` (unused).
10. **Ledger:** each received transfer writes `payment_received` with its own amount; refunds write `refund_issued`. `verifyPaymentLedgerInvariant` checks Σ received − Σ refunded = `amount` for `paid` and = 0 for `expired`. It still accepts old `deposit_received` entries (dev data). Ticket entries keep `ticket_received`.
11. **Draft lifetime:** `createBookingDraft` sets `payableUntil = createdAt + 30 min`. Each `createPayment` extends it to `max(payableUntil, expiresAt + 5 min)`. The sweep deletes drafts with `payableUntil <= now`, replacing `findUnpaidDraftsOlderThan` with `findExpiredDrafts(now)`. Old dev drafts without the field are not swept (ledger).
12. **At most 3 payments per booking** (`MAX_PAYMENT_ATTEMPTS = 3`), so "Tạo QR mới" cannot hold the day forever. The 4th call is refused with the new code `payment_attempts_exceeded` (Functions `failed-precondition`; app snackbar "Đã tạo QR 3 lần. Hãy đặt lại từ đầu.").
13. **Callables:** `createDeposit`/`checkDeposit` are replaced by `createPayment {bookingId, method}` and `checkPayment {bookingId}` (both removed from `index.ts`; the CI deploy uses `--force`). `confirmFakePayment {paymentId, kind?}` with `kind ∈ exact|under|over|wrong_content` (default `exact`) builds a signed body and runs the webhook handler in-process; guarded by `assertFakePaymentsAllowed` as today. Tickets keep `createDepositIntent`, `checkPaymentStatus` and `confirmFakeTicketPayment` until #137.
14. **Client mirror:** `bookings/{id}.checkout` = `{paymentId, method, amount, expiresAt (Timestamp), transferCode, attempt, qrPayload?, bankName?, accountMasked?, payUrl?, bankAppsUrl?}`, written by `createPayment`. The booking is readable by its parties; the masked collection account is not sensitive.
15. **Fake gateway instructions:**
    - `bank_qr` returns a real VietQR EMVCo payload for BIN `970418` (BIDV), account `96247000003812`, the amount and the transfer code, with `bankName "BIDV"`, `accountMasked "9624 •••• 3812"` and `bankAppsUrl "https://fake-pay.local/bank-apps"`.
    - Wallets return `payUrl "https://fake-pay.local/<method>?paymentId=…"`.
    - All of these are test values.
16. **"Mở app ngân hàng"** opens `checkout.bankAppsUrl` through `externalLauncherProvider` and is hidden when it is null. The real list of bank apps comes from the intermediary.
17. **S04.04 states:**
    - Expired (countdown at 0): QR dimmed with "QR đã hết hạn", the pill reads "Hết hạn", and the footer has a single **primary** "Tạo QR mới".
    - Wallet methods: the waiting view (`SignatureLoader` vibration, "Đang chờ xác nhận"), primary "Mở lại {MoMo|ZaloPay}" (opens `payUrl`) and outline "Kiểm tra lại" (`checkPayment`).
    - "Đổi cổng thanh toán" is removed (not in the mock).
    - In fake mode (`!realPayments`) a text button "Giả lập chuyển khoản" opens the reshaped `FakePaymentSheet` (the four kinds).
    - The booking sheet no longer opens the fake sheet before navigating.
18. **S04.03 layout follows the mock:** one helper line (`bookPolicy` carries the whole mock sentence) and no separate `EscrowNotice`. `bookRemaining` and `escrowNoticeDeposit` are deleted if nothing else uses them. The radio list is a new core widget, `PayMethodSelector`; `PayMethodPicker` stays for S11.03.

## Review Focus

1. A customer pays in two parts before expiry (under, then a top-up to the exact amount): the payment becomes paid, there is one booking transition, and nothing is refunded. The second part pushing the total over the amount: paid, and only the excess is refunded.
2. Bank apps mangle the content ("pb7f3k2q chuyen tien", "CK PB-7F3K2Q", trailing text): the transfer still matches. Content with no valid code goes to `needs_review` and does not crash.
3. The same transaction arrives by webhook twice, and the reconciliation job finds it again: one `payment_received` entry, one booking event, one refund at most.
4. After "Tạo QR mới" the customer pays the OLD QR: refunded in full (`extra_transfer` or `late_transfer`), the new QR stays valid, and the booking is never paid twice.
5. The countdown reaches 00:00 while the screen is open and before any sweep runs: the UI shows the expired state with only "Tạo QR mới". On the server, a transfer whose `receivedAt` is past `expiresAt` is refunded even though the status is still `created`.
