> **Detail level ("ở giữa", owner):** exact interfaces, tests listed by name with expected behaviour, sample code only for the hard parts.

**Goal:** A venue (for example Bãi đá sông Hồng) becomes an **organizer** account: it claims the management of an S29 spot (staff-approved), publishes reference entry fees, keeps a list of resident photographers whose guests enter free, sells **entry tickets** per visit day through the app's payment rails, invites gate staff and scans QR codes at the gate. Customers and providers see the entry fee before booking; a booking at a ticketed spot carries the tickets in the same VietQR payment; every paid booking or ticket has one **pass** (QR) that the right scanner resolves: the photographer's scan moves the booking to `shooting`, the gate's scan checks the ticket in. Events must declare their venue (managed spot / paid place / public) and whether the entry fee is included.

**Architecture:** Pure rules in `packages/domain`: `organizer.ts` + `organizer_use_cases.ts` (organizer profile, spot claims, residents with waivers, gate staff), `entry_tickets.ts` (entry events, open days, prices, daily capacity, refund cutoff, release per visit day), `group_settlement.ts` (a group payment that the customer pays once and that fans out into one held child payment per line: booking, entry tickets, event ticket, venue fee), `pass.ts` + `pass_scan.ts` (pass records and the scanner-role resolution), `booking_machine.ts` extended with `shooting`, `shoot_question.ts` (the "Buổi chụp có diễn ra không?" flow that replaces the automatic `mark_shot`), `event_venue.ts` (venue block, venue consent on the `hostConsent` mechanism, venue fee). Functions stay thin: 14 new callables (9 organizer, 3 entry/venue, 2 scan/shoot), adapters (`organizer_firestore.ts`, extensions of `event_firestore.ts`, `booking_firestore.ts`, `payment_firestore.ts`), rules, indexes, the `spots` CLI gains `claims` and `access`, and three existing clocks gain steps (no new Scheduler job). Flutter: `lib/data/organizer/`, `lib/data/passes/`, `lib/core/scan/` (the `PassScanner` port over `mobile_scanner`), `lib/features/organizer/` (S30.01–S30.04, S30.06), `lib/features/entry/` (S30.05), `lib/features/scan/` (S30.07), and the variants on S29.02, S04.03, S05.02, S06.01, S11.02/S11.03/S11.04, S12.01, S12.03, S09.02.

## Decisions

1. **Blocker and base.** #194 is hard (Execution order). Everything that touches `Spot`, `tools/spots.ts`, `spot_firestore.ts`, the S29.02 page or `Booking.spotId` extends #194's code in place, never redefines it.
2. **Organizer is a document, not a role.** `organizers/{uid}` `{name, bio, avatarUrl, status: active | suspended, phoneVerified, businessVerified, payoutAccountOk, createdAt, updatedAt}`. Any signed-in user with a verified phone may apply (`becomeOrganizer`); S01.05 is untouched. `EventHost.type` gains `'organizer'`; `isEventManager` treats `host.type === 'organizer' && host.id === caller.uid` as a manager (gate staff are **not** managers: they only scan, Decision 9). Staff suspend with the `spots` CLI (`organizers suspend <uid> --actor`): every open event of the organizer is cancelled through `cancelEventWithRefunds` (100%).
3. **Selling needs a business, and today only studios can get one.** `canSell(o) = status === 'active' && businessVerified && payoutAccountOk`. On `develop`, S21.04 business verification accepts only a verified studio provider (`business_verification.ts:60–63`) and its approval writes `photographers/{uid}` (`verification_firestore.ts:390–406`); saving a payout account requires the `photographer` role (`save_payout_account.ts:98`, `payout_account_use_cases.ts:52, 105`). This plan opens both to organizers (Task 1b): `submitBusinessVerification` accepts a caller with an `active` organizer document (no studio or identity requirement beyond the verified phone; the business record's `subject` becomes `'studio' | 'organizer'`), the approval writer patches `organizers/{uid}.businessVerified` (and `photographers/{uid}` only for a studio), and the payout-account use cases accept a caller who is a provider **or** an active organizer; for an organizer the holder name must match the verified business legal name (`holderNameKey`, the same S06.06 check), which sets `organizers/{uid}.payoutAccountOk`. Without `canSell`, `publishEvent(type: entry)`, priced events by an organizer, and `venueFeePerGuest > 0` are `business_required`; free events need only `active`.
4. **Spot claims are the only staff-reviewed documents.** `spot_claims/{id}` `{spotId, organizerId, status: pending | approved | rejected, proofPaths[] (1–3), note, reason?, createdAt, reviewedAt?, reviewedBy?}`; one `pending` claim per spot and per organizer; `spots.managedBy` is set on approval inside the same transaction and **only when still null** (`spot_managed` otherwise, the race pin of Review Focus 5). Proofs live in Storage `spot_claims/{uid}/{claimId}/{file}` (PDF or JPEG/PNG ≤ 10 MB, uploaded by the claimant before `claimSpot`, readable by claimant and staff; the uid segment is what lets Storage rules check the uploader). Staff review with `npm run spots -- claims list | approve <id> | reject <id> --reason … --actor <uid>` (Phase 1; S23 later). A spot proposal ("Gửi đề xuất điểm mới") is a `spot_claims` row with `spotId: null`, `proposal: {name, address}`; staff add the spot with `spots add` and approve the claim.
5. **Residents and waivers.** `spots/{spotId}/residents/{providerId}` `{waivesProvider, waivesGuests, maxGuests?, validUntil?, addedAt, addedBy}`; only `acceptsOrders` providers; ≤ 50; readable by everyone; `waiverFor(resident, now) → {provider: boolean, guests: boolean, maxGuests: number | null}` returns no waiver after `validUntil`. The daily `rankSpots` job (#194) gains `expireResidents` (delete rows with `validUntil < today`), so S29.02 and S04.03 never apply an expired waiver even before the job runs (the predicate checks the date too).
6. **Payment lines = a group payment that fans out.** Today a transfer code belongs to exactly one payment: `newSubjectPayment` derives `transferCode` from the payment id (`create_payment.ts:70`), `freshPaymentId` refuses a code in use (`:81–92`), `matchPayment` returns one payment (`payment_settlement.ts:231–251`), `applyTransfer` is a pure function over one payment (`:93–156`), and `settleTransfer` hands it to the one `SubjectSettler` of its `subjectType`, which runs in that subject's own store transaction (`:276–283`, `payment_ports.ts:28–35`). So the spec's "one VietQR, several held amounts" (§5.2) is built as:
   - `PAYMENT_SUBJECTS` gains `'payment_group'`. A **group payment** `payments/{gid}` (`subjectType: 'payment_group'`, `subjectId: gid`, `payerId`, `payeeId: payerId` (no payee; never released), `amount = Σ lines`, its own `transferCode` from its id as today) is the only thing the customer pays. `issuePaymentInstructions`, `matchPayment`, `applyTransfer`, the partial/over/late rules and `confirmFakePayment` (which fakes `amount` of the matched payment, `:310`) all work on it unchanged.
   - Each **line** is a normal child Payment (`booking`, or `event_registration` for an entry ticket, an event ticket or the venue fee) with `groupId = gid`, `lineKind`, and `status: 'awaiting_group'` (new `PAYMENT_STATUSES` value: not payable on its own, no transfer code issued, skipped by `findPaymentsToReconcile`/`findOverduePayments`/`matchPayment`). `payment_groups/{gid}` `{payerId, lines: [{paymentId, kind, subjectType, subjectId, payeeId, amount}], total, status, expiresAt}` is the read model the app shows.
   - A new `SubjectSettler` for `'payment_group'` (`group_settlement.ts`) runs **one** transaction over a `GroupSettlementTx` port that can read and write payments, bookings, registrations, `entry_days` and passes (the Functions adapter implements it over one Firestore transaction; the memory adapter over the booking and event memory stores sharing one lock). On `paid`: the group payment becomes `paid` with `escrowStatus: 'released'` and `payableAmount: 0` (its money is now the children's), every child becomes `paid` with the subject's paid effects (booking: the existing booking-paid path incl. `depositPaidAt` and contact unlock; registration: the existing ticket-paid path incl. `entry_days` held → sold), each child gets its own `escrowStatus: 'held'` and `releaseAfter` by its rule, and one pass is created (Decision 7). On `partial`/`over`/`late`: the existing outcomes on the group payment (refund the transfer); children stay `awaiting_group`. `expire`: the group payment expires as today and every child expires with it, releasing the held registration and the booking's draft hold (spec §5.2 last bullet). `confirmFakePayment` on a booking or registration that has a `groupId` confirms the group.
   - Milestones, payouts, refunds, ledger invariants and earnings keep working per child Payment with no change. A group exists only when there are ≥ 2 lines; a single line keeps today's direct payment. Needs the owner 1 records this reading of §5.2.
7. **Pass codes are new random codes.** Ticket codes are `VE-XXXX-MMDD`: only 4 random characters, the last 4 are the date (`ticket_code.ts:7, 15–22`; `isTicketCode` requires `\d{4}`), so they cannot serve as the meeting proof. A pass code is **8 random characters** from `TRANSFER_CODE_ALPHABET` (`payment_code.ts:8`, the spec's alphabet `23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ`, 30^8 ≈ 6.6·10¹¹) drawn with `deps.random`, retried on collision (`passes/{code}` exists), shown as `VE-XXXX-XXXX` (`passDisplay`), validated by `isPassCode` (`/^(VE-)?[2-9A-HJ-NP-TV-Z]{4}-?[2-9A-HJ-NP-TV-Z]{4}$/` after uppercasing). `passCode4`/`ticketCodeLast4` are the last 4 **random** characters. `passes/{code8}` `{customerId, day, bookingId?, registrationIds[], createdAt}`. A booking with tickets shares one pass (spec §6.1): created when the group is paid; a booking without tickets when its payment is paid; a lone entry ticket when its registration is paid. `scanPass` also accepts a legacy `VE-XXXX-MMDD` ticket code and resolves it through the existing `ticket_codes` index (`TICKET_CODES` in `event_firestore.ts`), so the deleted old scanner loses nothing.
8. **Pass privacy.** New registrations no longer write the public `ticketCode`: they write `ticketCodeLast4` and `registrations/{id}/private/pass {code}`; bookings write `bookings/{id}/private/pass {code}` (rules: the customer only). Old registrations keep their public `ticketCode` (legacy, read-only); the app prefers `private/pass` and falls back to `ticketCode`. S12.03's attendee list shows `ticketCodeLast4` (or the last 4 of a legacy code).
9. **Scanning is resolved by the server from the scanner's roles.** `scanPass({code})` loads the pass (or a legacy ticket code through `ticket_codes`, Decision 7), then in one transaction computes `resolveScan(parts, scanner, now)` with the `Scanner` of the Contract (`{uid, shooterOf, managerOf, gateOf, managesSpots}`; the adapter collects only what the pass's parts need: the pass's booking, the pass's registrations' events, the scanner's active `gate_staff` rows and managed spots) and writes the updates plus `entry_days.checkedIn` and the gate staff's `scansToday {day, count}`; the result is the list of allowed updates (`booking → shooting` with `checkedInAt`; `registration.checkedInAt`; `registration.gateCheckedInAt`) or an error (`not_your_pass`, `wrong_day`, `too_early`, `already_checked_in`, `refunded`). A scanner updates only their own parts; the response lists what changed (`{booking?: {id, customerName}, registrations: [{id, eventTitle, quantity, kind}]}`). `checkInRegistration` stays as a callable for old clients and delegates to `scanPass` with the event manager role only.
10. **`shooting` and the shoot question.** `BOOKING_STATUSES` adds `'shooting'` after `'upcoming'`; `TransitionAction` adds `'scan'` (photographer or studio assignee, `upcoming → shooting`, window = booking day from `start − 2 h` to `end + CHECKIN_GRACE_HOURS`) and `mark_shot` by the photographer now requires `shooting` when the booking has a pass (spec §6.3, Decision 14). The sweep step "auto mark-shot 24 h after end" (`booking_sweeps.ts:76–90`, its only caller of `findAutoCompletions`) changes: an **`upcoming`** booking at `end + 24 h` gets the shoot question (`shootQuestionAt = now`, `shootQuestionUntil = now + 48 h`, customer notification `shoot_question`); a **`shooting`** booking at `end + 24 h` is moved to `shot` by the system as before (the scan already proved the meeting; Needs the owner 5). `answerShootHappened({bookingId, happened})`: `true` → `shot` with `shotAt = endsAt` (milestones split as today); `false` → `shootDeniedAt = now` and the app opens S18.01 with the reason preset ("Buổi chụp không diễn ra"), the booking stays `upcoming` with its money held until the dispute; no answer by `shootQuestionUntil` → `shot` (sweep). A `shooting` booking cannot be cancelled (`not_eligible`) and the cancel refund table still keys on the start time for `upcoming` (spec §6.3). **`shooting` joins every status list where `upcoming` means "active"**: contact unlock (`contact.ts:75–83`, `firestore.rules:460` `isBookingContactUnlocked`, app `contact_access.dart:18`), disputes (`open_dispute.ts:46`: allowed any time while `shooting`), reminders, the app's `BookingStatus` switches, timeline, S05.01 buckets and S06.01 "today"; Task 4 Step 1 greps `'upcoming'` and `BookingStatus.upcoming` across `packages/domain/src`, `app_flutter/firebase/functions/src`, `firestore.rules` and `app_flutter/lib` and ledgers each site with the decision taken.
11. **Entry events are events with their own rules.** `EVENT_TYPES` adds `'entry'`; an entry event has `host.type = 'organizer'`, `spotId` (must equal a spot the organizer manages), `startAt/endAt` = the sale range (≤ 180 days), `closedWeekdays`, `closedDays`, `openHours {start, end}`, `ticketTypes [{id, name, price, hidden}]`, `dailyCapacity`, `refundCutoffHours`; `location` = the spot (`{name: spot.name, meetingPoint: '', lat, lng, geohash, areaId}` from #194's `Spot`), `coverUrl` = the spot's cover, `dayKey` = null; `capacity`, `price`, `hashtag*` (empty string, `hashtagLocked: true`), `chatId`, `faq`, `registrationDeadline` are null/empty. Every place that assumes a one-day, seat-counted event branches on `type === 'entry'`:
   - `saveEventDraft`/`publishEvent`/`updateEvent` validate with `validateEntryEvent` instead of `validateEventFields` (which rejects multi-day ranges, `event.ts:358–361`); `publishEvent` creates **no** group chat (`event_use_cases.ts:225–238`) and marks **no** host availability.

   - `activeRegistrationOf` (one active registration per user per event, `event_registration.ts:124–130`) is keyed by `(eventId, userId, visitDay)` for entry events, so a customer can hold tickets for several days, and a booking-attached registration (`bookingId` set) is ignored by the lone-ticket check.
   - Public lists exclude entry events: S11.01 and nearby queries add `type != 'entry'` (`firestore_event_repository.dart:63`, `firestore_nearby_events_repository.dart:24`; Functions-side lists likewise), so they never show as "Không thu phí".

## Review Focus

1. **Two buyers take the last seats of a day.** Both read `entry_days` `{sold 8, held 0}` with capacity 10 and buy 2. Pinned: Task 2 `two concurrent registerEntry for the last seats: exactly one succeeds, the other is sold_out_day` (memory store serializes; `entry_days` is read and written in the registration transaction), Task 6 `FirestoreEventStore reads and writes entry_days inside the registration transaction` (fake Firestore records reads), integration test (written) `parallel registerEntry on the last seats → one sold_out_day`.
2. **The wrong scanner changes the wrong part.** A photographer scans a pass that also carries tickets; a gate scans a pass that carries a booking; a gate staff of another organizer scans a ticket. Pinned: Task 4 `the shooter's scan moves the booking only and leaves registrations untouched`, `the gate's scan checks the registration in and never touches the booking`, `gate staff of organizer B scanning A's ticket is not_your_pass`, `a normal event manager scanning an entry ticket is not_your_pass`, `an event manager of event X scanning event Y's ticket is not_your_pass`; rules test `gate staff cannot read registrations/*/private/contact`.
3. **The pass leaks.** A provider, host or staff member reads the customer's code and "scans" without meeting them. Pinned: Task 6 rules tests `the photographer cannot read bookings/{id}/private/pass`, `the host cannot read registrations/{id}/private/pass`, `nobody reads passes/*`, `registrationToFirestore writes ticketCodeLast4 and no ticketCode for a new registration`; Task 4 `scanPass responses never include the customer's phone`.
4. **An under-paid group marks one line paid.** The customer transfers 290,000 for a 300,000 group (booking + tickets). Pinned: Task 3 `below total → partial on the group only, children untouched`, `expire → group and children expired, held registration released`, `exact total → … one pass`; Task 6 `the group settler's Firestore transaction … leaves the children awaiting_group`.
5. **Two organizers claim one spot, or a claim is approved twice.** Pinned: Task 1 `two pending claims on one spot: the second claimSpot is limit_exceeded only for the same organizer, allowed for another; the second approval is spot_managed and writes nothing`, `approving a claim whose spot got a manager meanwhile is spot_managed` (transaction re-reads `managedBy`).
